
# gregoryjoshua.com - Personal Website

A personal website I built to host my portfolio, record some of my favorite hobbies and interests, and provide a means of contact for anyone who would like to chat.


## Table of Contents
- [Overview](#overview)
- [Architecture](#architecture)
- [Reproducibility Steps](#reproducibility-steps)


## Overview

### Motivation and Goal
Different facets of life (career, interests, connections, etc.) are documented on different platforms and shared with different audiences. I wanted to build something that centralizes information about me in a way that depicts my own personality and style. I started this project with the goal that every aspect of it, from the content itself to details like the font, represents my character.

### Design
The design for this website started with the picture. Once I selected the picture of myself posing on a mountain in Moreno Valley, I chose the glass theme, as I thought it complemented the vibrant, fresh nature of the photo. From there, I designed a layout that was able to effectively communicate information while also maintaining the glassy, vibrant theme.

## Architecture

### Pages
- Home: An introductory landing page that states the purpose of the site.
- Portfolio: Details my career summary and projects.
- Hobbies: Describes my hobbies and interests.
- Contact: A platform to message me.
